## The offer worth understanding

Sakara does not frame this as a discount at all: one-time orders simply cost
**20% more per meal** than the same programme on subscription. At these prices
that is hundreds of dollars a month on the five-day programme, which makes it
the single most consequential choice in the checkout. The page treats it as the
headline offer for that reason.

## Editorial position

The page states the monthly cost plainly and says Sakara competes with eating
out rather than with groceries. At $676-$1,396 a month, a page that soft-pedals
the price is not serving the reader.
