# Vahdam Teas — internal notes

Not published. See `_notes/README.md`.

## This is the weakest-sourced page on the site

Three of four offers are marked `status: unverified` on purpose. Vahdam runs
separate US, UK, German and Indian storefronts with different terms, and the
sources contradict each other:

- **Free shipping** — cited at $49 in some places and $74.99 in others, for
  different regions. No figure is printed on the page; it tells the reader to
  check their own storefront instead.
- **Subscribe & Save 15%** — advertised, but sources disagree over whether it
  applies to the first order only or recurs. Marked unverified.
- **Referral** — the documented reward (Rs 150 to each party) is the Indian
  storefront's. No US equivalent confirmed.

Confirmed: founder Bala Sarda, founded 2015 at age 23, VAHDAM is an anagram of
his father's name Madhav, ~175 plantations across seven Indian tea regions,
storefronts in US/CA/UK/DE, and the first-time-buyer refund of up to $35 with no
returns once packaging is opened.

## Rejected claims

Vahdam codes advertised between 20% and 70% off across aggregators — none
confirmable against a Vahdam-owned page, and Vahdam's own frequent sitewide
sales make many of them redundant anyway.

## Next pass — priority

This page needs a proper pass against the **US storefront specifically**. Settle
the shipping threshold and the subscription recurrence and promote both offers
out of `unverified`, or drop them. Until then the page leans on the refund
guarantee as its headline, which is thinner than the other merchants.
