## Affiliate note

AG1's programme is **selective**, and its promotions almost always bundle extra
items with a first subscription order rather than discounting the price. Two
consequences:

1. If our tracking link carries a specific Welcome Kit bundle, the kit contents
   described on the page must match what our link actually delivers. Check
   before setting `status: active`.
2. The page deliberately describes the scoop, shaker and canister as the
   reliable core and says additional items rotate — so it stays accurate as
   promotions change.

## Editorial position

This is the most heavily marketed brand on the site and the page pushes back
hardest: it states plainly that AG1 is expensive, broad rather than clinically
dosed, and only worth it if it genuinely replaces several products. Keep that.
A page that reads like the podcast ads is worth nothing to a reader.
