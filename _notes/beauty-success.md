## The offer the site under-advertises

**Free click and collect with no minimum.** Beauty Success foregrounds the €60
home-delivery threshold, but collection in store is free at any basket size.
That is the headline offer on our page because it is the one that actually
changes a small order, and it is the advantage a store network has over pure
e-commerce.

## Editorial note

Selective perfumery is price-controlled by the brands, so this retailer cannot
undercut other approved sellers by much on the bottles themselves. The page says
so rather than implying deals that do not exist, and points instead at the
authenticity guarantee — counterfeit and grey-market fragrance is a genuine
problem on marketplaces, so approved-distributor status is real value.
