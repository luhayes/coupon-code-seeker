## Two things the page calls out deliberately

**The $5 return fee.** This is a less generous policy than most merchants we
carry and it is stated plainly, including the arithmetic — on a $95 bag it is
over 5% of the purchase. Readers ordering two sizes to compare should know
before, not after.

**Wide retail distribution.** The same bags sell through QVC, Macy's and Amazon,
so the brand's own site is not automatically cheapest. The page says so. That is
uncomfortable for affiliate revenue and correct for the reader; keep it.
