## About Beauty Success

The business began in **1973**, when Nicole Georges opened a perfumery under her own name in Périgueux. The Beauty Success banner itself arrived in **1995** with the first branded store, and the chain passed 100 locations by the early 2000s. In **October 2022** the parent company renamed itself **Groupe NOVI**, keeping Beauty Success as its consumer-facing brand — so you will see both names, and they are the same company. The group runs roughly **650 stores across 15 countries**, of which about **310 are Beauty Success**, with around 2,700 staff and revenue near €245–250 million.

The commercially useful fact for a shopper is the distribution status: Beauty Success has been an **approved distributor of selective perfumery brands for 40 years** and guarantees authenticity on everything it sells. In a category where marketplace listings and grey-market imports are a genuine problem — counterfeit and diverted fragrance is widespread online — buying from an approved distributor is worth something real, and it is the main argument for using a chain like this over a cheaper marketplace seller.

Positioning honestly: selective perfumery is price-controlled by the brands, so Beauty Success cannot undercut other approved retailers on the fragrances themselves by much. Where it competes is on the loyalty programme, delivery convenience and the store network — including beauty institutes offering treatments, which pure e-commerce cannot match. If you are hunting the lowest possible price on a specific bottle, this is not automatically it; if you want a guaranteed-genuine product with points accruing across online and in-store purchases, it is a sound default.
