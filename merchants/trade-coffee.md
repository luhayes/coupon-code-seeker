## About Trade Coffee

The problem Trade set out to solve is discovery. Specialty coffee is dominated by small roasters whose best work never reaches anyone outside their city, while the customer standing in front of a shelf has no way to tell a bag they will love from one they will not. Trade sits in between: it holds the relationships with hundreds of roasters, takes your brew method and taste preferences, and picks for you — then adjusts as you rate what arrives.

The freshness argument is real and is the main advantage over supermarket coffee. Bags are **roasted after you order and shipped within 48 hours**, where retail coffee has often been sitting for months. For drip, pour-over and espresso drinkers who grind at home, that difference is obvious in the cup.

Positioning honestly: you are paying specialty prices, and the matching is only as good as the quiz plus your feedback — the first bag is sometimes a miss. Trade clearly knows this, which is why the **First-Bag Guarantee** exists, and it is the reason to start a subscription rather than buy a single bag: the algorithm needs a few rounds of ratings before it is genuinely dialled in.
