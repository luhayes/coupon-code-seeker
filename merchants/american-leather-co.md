## About American Leather Co.

The brand's whole proposition sits in one material choice: **glove leather**. It is the soft, pliable leather traditionally used for gloves, and it gives these bags a slouch and a hand-feel that stiffer corrected-grain leather does not have at this price. Paired with antiqued hardware and fairly classic silhouettes, the result reads as more expensive than it is.

Most bags land between **$95 and $195**, which is the interesting part of the positioning. That sits above fast-fashion PU bags and well below entry designer leather — a band where genuinely few brands compete, because real leather at $150 requires either scale or thin margins. American Leather Co. gets there partly through wide distribution: the same bags appear at **QVC, Macy's and Amazon** as well as the brand's own site.

That distribution is worth knowing before you buy, and it cuts both ways. It means you can often find a specific style discounted somewhere in the retail chain. It also means the brand's own site is not automatically the cheapest place to buy — though it is the only place that reliably carries the full range and the current-season colours.

Positioning honestly: this is soft, unstructured leather, so bags slouch rather than hold a shape, and glove leather scuffs more readily than a coated or pebbled finish. If you want a bag that stands up on its own and shrugs off scratches, look elsewhere. If you want something that softens and ages, this is the point.
