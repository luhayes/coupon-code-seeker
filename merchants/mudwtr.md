## About MUD\WTR

Shane Heath founded MUD\WTR in 2018 after what he has described as years of heavy caffeination that looked productive from the outside and felt like anxiety from the inside. The product is his answer to that: a drink with enough lift to replace the morning coffee ritual without the jitters that pushed him off coffee in the first place.

The flagship blend, **:rise**, combines masala chai, cacao, turmeric, cinnamon, lion's mane and chaga, and has passed 130 million servings sold. The range is organised around the shape of a day rather than around flavours — one blend to start the morning, one to reset in the afternoon, one to wind down at night — which is also the mechanism that makes the subscription make sense, since the brand is selling a daily habit rather than an occasional treat.

Positioning honestly: this is a premium product in a category where instant coffee costs a fraction as much, and the taste is genuinely divisive — earthy and spiced rather than coffee-like, so people expecting coffee are often disappointed. The 30-day guarantee covering opened product exists precisely because the brand knows the first cup decides it, and that makes a first order unusually low-risk to try.
