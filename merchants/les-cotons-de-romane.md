## About Les Cotons de Romane

**Romane Gautret** started the business in **2019**. She trained and worked as a paediatric nurse, and the range grew out of that: reusable alternatives to the single-use cotton, wipes and pads that households get through constantly. Everything in the sewn range is made by the brand in **Rennes**, in Brittany, rather than sourced from a supplier and relabelled — which is the actual difference between this and the large volume of generic "zero waste" sets sold on marketplaces.

The brand built its audience on TikTok and Instagram, where Romane posts as the maker rather than through an agency, and it sells wholesale to independent shops through Ankorstore as well as direct. It has also run occasional open-door sales at its Rennes workshop.

Positioning honestly: this is a small maker, not a retailer, and the economics follow from that. There is no subscription programme, no loyalty scheme and no standing discount structure — the direct price is simply the price, and it is higher than a generic set of reusable pads from a marketplace. What you are paying for is a known maker, French manufacture and fabric quality. If your priority is the lowest possible price per pad, this is not the cheapest route and the brand does not claim to be.
