## About Seed

Seed Health was co-founded by **Ara Katz**, who serves as co-CEO, as a microbiome-science company rather than a supplement brand — a distinction the company works hard to maintain. It publishes clinical data, has presented DS-01 research at Digestive Disease Week, and is unusually specific about strain identity and dosing in a category where most products list a genus, a species and a CFU count and leave it there.

That rigour is the reason to buy it and also the reason it is expensive. **DS-01 costs $49.99 a month**, several times what a supermarket probiotic costs, and probiotics remain a field where individual response varies enormously — the strains that help one person may do nothing for another regardless of how well studied they are. Seed's answer is the refund policy: try a month, and if nothing changes, get the product cost back.

The packaging is also part of the pitch. The first month ships as a **Welcome Kit** with a reusable glass jar and a travel vial; every refill afterwards arrives in compostable packaging designed to be poured into the jar you already have. It is a real reduction in plastic, and it also locks you gently into the subscription, since refills assume you kept the jar.
