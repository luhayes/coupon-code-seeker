## About Pique Life

Pique was founded in 2014 by **Simon Cheng**, whose stated goal was to source the highest-quality functional ingredients available and deliver them in a form people would actually drink every day. Rather than selling loose leaf or teabags, Pique cold-extracts whole leaves over several hours and crystallizes the result, so a sachet dissolves instantly in a glass of cold water — the format that made the brand recognizable.

The brand leans hard on sourcing and purity as its differentiators. **Sun Goddess Matcha**, its flagship, is organic ceremonial-grade matcha shade-grown in Kagoshima, Japan and produced by third-generation tea masters using techniques the brand traces back roughly 130 years; Kagoshima sits at the southern tip of the archipelago, which Pique markets as the source farthest from industrial contamination. Products are triple toxin-screened for heavy metals, pesticides and toxic mold.

Pique sits at the premium end of the category — cartons generally run $50–$90 — so most of the real savings come from the subscription program, the loyalty scheme and bundles rather than from one-off coupon codes. It's a fit for shoppers who want a daily matcha, tea or electrolyte habit and will buy repeatedly; it's expensive as a one-time impulse purchase.
