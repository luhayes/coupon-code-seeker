## About Moon Juice

**Amanda Chantal Bacon** founded Moon Juice in **2012**, after putting an autoimmune condition into remission — something she had been told repeatedly was not possible. The brand began as a Los Angeles shop selling adaptogenic tonics and became, for a period, the most parodied name in wellness: this is the company behind the "dusts" that launched a thousand jokes about Goop-adjacent Los Angeles.

The interesting thing is what happened next. The current range is considerably more disciplined than the brand's reputation suggests. **SuperYou** is sold on a specific, measurable claim — a **24% reduction in cortisol** — rather than on vibes, and **Magnesi-Om** combines three bioavailable magnesium forms with L-theanine, which is a defensible formulation rather than a novelty. The packaging still looks like the 2016 wellness boom; the formulas underneath have grown up.

Positioning honestly: adaptogens remain a category where the clinical literature is thinner than the marketing, and Moon Juice prices at the premium end of it. The 45-day return window with a prepaid label is unusually generous for supplements and is the reason a first order is low-risk — you have long enough to actually judge whether anything changed.
