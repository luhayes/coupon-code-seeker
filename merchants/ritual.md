## About Ritual

Katerina Schneider founded Ritual after searching for a prenatal vitamin while pregnant and finding she could not determine what was actually in the products on the shelf, or where it came from. The company was built around fixing that specific problem, which is why traceability — naming the supplier behind each ingredient — became the brand's central claim rather than a marketing afterthought.

The product design follows from it: a small number of ingredients the company argues most people are actually short of, rather than a long label of everything at token doses. The delayed-release capsule is meant to dissolve later in the digestive tract, which is Ritual's answer to the nausea that puts people off taking vitamins with breakfast.

Positioning honestly: at roughly **$30–$35 a bottle**, Ritual costs several times what a supermarket multivitamin does, and the clinical case for a healthy adult taking any multivitamin at all is contested. What you are paying for is transparency about sourcing and a formulation that is deliberately narrow. If that matters to you it is a reasonable purchase; if you simply want cheap coverage, it is not the value option, and the brand does not really claim to be.
