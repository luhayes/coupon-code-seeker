## About AG1

AG1 is the most heavily marketed supplement of the past decade, and its podcast sponsorships are the reason most people have heard of it. That ubiquity cuts both ways: it means near-universal name recognition, and it means the product is judged more harshly than its formulation alone would warrant.

The proposition is consolidation. Rather than buying a multivitamin, a greens powder, a probiotic and a handful of adaptogens separately, you take one scoop that covers the ground approximately. For people who were genuinely buying four or five separate products, that is a real simplification and the price comparison is closer than it first appears.

Positioning honestly — and this is the brand where it matters most, because the marketing is relentless: **at roughly $79 a month on subscription, AG1 is among the most expensive daily supplements you can buy.** It is a broad, moderately dosed formula rather than a clinically dosed one; for any single ingredient, a standalone supplement will give you more for less. The reason to buy it is adherence, not potency — one scoop you actually take every morning beats five bottles you stop opening. If you are not already spending comparable money across several products, the honest answer is that this is a lot to pay for convenience.

The **90-day money-back guarantee** is unusually long and is the right way to settle the question for yourself.
