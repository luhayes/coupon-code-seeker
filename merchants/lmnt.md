## About LMNT

LMNT was founded in **late 2019** by Robb Wolf, James Murphy, Tyler Cartwright and Luis Villaseñor. Wolf is a former research biochemist known in ancestral-health circles; Villaseñor founded Ketogains. The formula came out of a specific observation rather than a marketing brief: people on low-carb diets shed sodium quickly and feel terrible for reasons usually misattributed to carb withdrawal.

That heritage explains the product's most divisive feature. At **1,000mg of sodium per stick**, LMNT is several times saltier than mainstream electrolyte tablets, and it tastes it. People who need that dose describe it as the only mix that works; people who came looking for a flavoured hydration drink often find it aggressively salty. Try the Sample Pack before committing to a box of one flavour — which, conveniently, is exactly what the standing free-sample offer is designed for.

Pricing is straightforward: roughly **$45 for a box of 30 sticks**, around $1.50 a serving, with shipping already inside that price. That is expensive next to a generic electrolyte tablet and competitive against other premium mixes.
